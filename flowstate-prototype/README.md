# FLOWSTATE
### A supply chain improvement concept by Dania Sami

I built FLOWSTATE to explore how connected operational data and practical improvement workflows could support faster, better decisions across e-commerce logistics.

My focus is simple: understand the flow from supplier to customer, spot friction, test improvement ideas, and help teams turn those ideas into measurable action. This first prototype demonstrates the concept through an operations overview, an interactive fulfillment map, a scenario-based business case calculator, and a shared improvement board.

## How to run

1. Unzip the folder.
2. Double-click `index.html` to open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
3. Explore the four sections using the left navigation. No installation, server, account, or API key is needed.

The improvement lab lets you change assumptions and export a business case as a text file. You can also add improvement initiatives to the team board and change their status. The board saves changes in your browser's local storage.

## What I would build next

I would validate the process assumptions with operational teams, connect real ERP and WMS event data where authorized, introduce historical trend analysis, and develop stronger capacity and delivery simulations. The goal is to evolve this from a clear demonstration into a useful decision-support tool.

**Important:** All operational figures, workflows, and scenarios in this prototype are illustrative. They are not Adlibris internal data or verified performance measurements. FLOWSTATE is an independent portfolio concept and is not an official Adlibris product.
